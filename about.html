About SKZ : UNVEIL

More than a pop-up. A space for STAY.

Created as a fictional experience concept, SKZ : UNVEIL celebrates creativity, community, and music-inspired connection.

Our Story

SKZ : UNVEIL was imagined as a fan-centered pop-up experience inspired by the energy and creativity associated with Stray Kids. The concept brings together immersive spaces, interactive moments, and collectible keepsakes so fans can engage with the event in a meaningful way.

The goal of the experience is to create an atmosphere that feels exciting, memorable, and thoughtfully designed for visitors who want to celebrate their connection to the music.

Our Mission

To create a welcoming, memorable, and visually engaging pop-up experience where STAY can explore, create, and celebrate together.

  • Creativity — encouraging self-expression.
  • Connection — building shared fan experiences.
  • Celebration — honoring the music and memories fans love.
  • Inclusivity — making the space welcoming for everyone.
  • Memories — designing moments people will remember.
