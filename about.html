About SKZ : UNVEIL

A space for Stray Kids and STAY.

SKZ : UNVEIL is designed to celebrate creativity, community, and the connection between the artists and fandom.

Our Story

SKZ : UNVEIL is an event company specializing in Stray Kids pop-up experiences, fan events, and exclusive collections. Our team designs memorable environments that bring music, storytelling, and community together. Designed with STAY in mind, the experience brings together interactive exhibits, themed installations, exclusive merchandise, and memorable moments that celebrate the group's artistic journey.

Every part of an event is designed to encourage exploration and connection. Visitors can discover unique photo opportunities, participate in interactive activities, browse limited-edition collections, and enjoy spaces inspired by the visuals and storytelling that have made Stray Kids known around the world.

STAY, the official fandom of Stray Kids, is at the center of every experience we create. Each event is designed to help fans connect, create memories, and celebrate their shared passion through meaningful activities and creative spaces.

More than just an event, SKZ : UNVEIL aims to create an environment where music, creativity, and community come together. Whether visitors are exploring the exhibits, collecting exclusive merchandise, or sharing the experience with fellow STAY, every moment is designed to feel welcoming, memorable, and inspiring.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create an unforgettable entertainment experience where STAY can celebrate music, creativity, and community through immersive spaces, interactive activities, and meaningful shared moments.

Our Values

  • Creativity — Inspiring imagination through immersive experiences and design.
  • Community — Bringing STAY together in a welcoming and inclusive environment.
  • Connection — Creating meaningful moments between fans, music, and memories.
  • Quality — Delivering carefully designed experiences with attention to every detail.
  • Inspiration — Encouraging visitors to celebrate creativity and express themselves.
