About SKZ : UNVEIL

More than a pop-up. A space for STAY.

SKZ : UNVEIL is an immersive pop-up experience designed to celebrate creativity, community, and the connection between music and STAY.

Our Story

SKZ : UNVEIL is a creative event company specializing in immersive pop-up experiences, fan events, and exclusive collections inspired by Stray Kids. Our team designs memorable environments that bring music, storytelling, and community together. Designed with STAY in mind, the experience brings together interactive exhibits, themed installations, exclusive merchandise, and memorable moments that celebrate the group's artistic journey.

Every part of the event is designed to encourage exploration and connection. Visitors can discover unique photo opportunities, participate in interactive activities, browse limited-edition collections, and enjoy spaces inspired by the visuals and storytelling that have made Stray Kids known around the world.

More than just an event, SKZ : UNVEIL aims to create an environment where music, creativity, and community come together. Whether visitors are exploring the exhibits, collecting exclusive merchandise, or sharing the experience with fellow STAY, every moment is designed to feel welcoming, memorable, and inspiring.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create an unforgettable entertainment experience where STAY can celebrate music, creativity, and community through immersive spaces, interactive activities, and meaningful shared moments.

Our Values

  • Creativity — Inspiring imagination through immersive experiences and design.
  • Community — Bringing STAY together in a welcoming and inclusive environment.
  • Connection — Creating meaningful moments between fans, music, and memories.
  • Quality — Delivering carefully designed experiences with attention to every detail.
  • Inspiration — Encouraging visitors to celebrate creativity and express themselves.
